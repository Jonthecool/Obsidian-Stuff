# Daily Supplement Routine

*Goals: Better sleep · Mental performance (LSAT prep) · Lower stress & anxiety · Reduce spiraling/rumination*

---

## Morning — With Breakfast

| Supplement                          | Why I Take It                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Ashwagandha**                     | Lowers cortisol and stress hormones. Reduces anxiety and improves sleep quality over time. One of the most researched adaptogens.                                                                |
| **Rhodiola Rosea**                  | Reduces mental and physical fatigue. Improves stress resilience and energy. Activating so must be taken in the morning only.                                                                     |
| **Force Factor Modern Mushrooms 7** | Lion's Mane stimulates nerve growth factor (NGF) for long-term memory and cognition. Cordyceps boosts ATP and oxygen efficiency for gym and mental stamina.                                      |
| **Creatine Monohydrate (5g)**       | Fuels ATP in the brain. Improves working memory, processing speed, and reduces mental fatigue. Critical for LSAT prep.                                                                           |
| **Vitamin D3 + K2**                 | D3 is essential for serotonin and dopamine production — low levels are strongly linked to anxiety and depression. K2 directs calcium properly and makes D3 safe long term. Take with fatty food. |
| **NAC (600mg)**                     | Regulates glutamate to reduce rumination and looping/spiraling thoughts. Antioxidant support for the brain. Builds over weeks.                                                                   |
| **Boron (10mg)**                     | Supports testosterone levels, cognitive function, and reduces inflammation. Taken with breakfast. Started June 4, 2026 — permanent morning addition.                                             |

## Stimulant Stack Protocol (Adderall + Caffeine)

*Tested and refined: week of June 2, 2026*

### Daily Order on Adderall Days
- **Option A — Coffee First (Recommended):** Coffee + L-Theanine (200mg) → wait 30-60 min → Adderall
  - Caffeine peak tapers before Adderall peaks → staggered, smoother, less overlap
- **Option B — Adderall First (Stronger peak):** Adderall + L-Theanine → coffee 1-2 hrs later
  - Peaks overlap more; use when maximum intensity is needed and stimulants are well-tolerated

### If Other Supps Already Taken Before Coffee
- Wait 30-45 min after supps → coffee → Adderall 30-60 min after coffee

### Rules
- Max **1 cup coffee** on Adderall days to avoid anxiety/overstimulation
- **L-Theanine (200mg) always paired with Adderall** — smooths jitteriness, no focus blunting
- **Rhodiola on separate days from Adderall** (see Key Rules)
- No heart palpitations expected when staggering correctly — monitor HR/anxiety as signal

### Treadmill / Come-Up Hack
- During Adderall come-up (first 30-60 min): do 20-30 min incline walk or light cardio
- Channels physical restlessness → sit down at peak (~2-3 hrs post-dose) for cleaner sustained focus
- Especially useful on LSAT study days

### XR Release Pattern (20mg)
- First release: ~30-60 min post-dose (onset, jittery phase)
- Second release: ~4 hrs post-dose (sustains the peak)
- Peak window: roughly hrs 2-5 post-dose
- Comedown: gradual fade hrs 5-7; eat real meal, take magnesium + ashwagandha

### Exam Day Strategy
- Replicate practice stack exactly — no new additions on test day
- Optional: Cut caffeine 5-7 days before exam to reset tolerance; use one targeted cup on test day for amplified effect (withdrawal period is rough — plan during lighter prep week)

## Key Rules
- **Rhodiola strictly morning only** — activating, will disrupt sleep if taken late
- **Rhodiola + Adderall: do NOT combine same day** — both activate dopamine/norepinephrine pathways; stacking increases anxiety and total stimulant load. Use Rhodiola on non-Adderall days for stress resilience and focus. If unavoidable, take Rhodiola at half dose (150mg) early only
- **D3 + K2 with a fatty meal** for proper absorption
- **NAC and Ashwagandha always with food** to avoid nausea
- **Nothing new on LSAT test day**
- **Cycle Ashwagandha and Rhodiola** every 8 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off to maintain sensitivity
