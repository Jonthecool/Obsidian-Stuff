# Error Log

> Your error log is your most valuable study tool. Patterns here tell you exactly where to focus.
> Back to [[LSAT Prep Hub]]

---

## How to Use This Log

After every practice session or PT:
1. Log **every question you missed or guessed on**
2. Write down what you *thought* the answer was and why
3. Write down what went wrong (wrong question type read? missed the conclusion? fell for a trap?)
4. Identify the **pattern** — are you consistently missing Flaw questions? RC Inference? Weaken?

## Pattern Analysis

*(Update monthly or after every 3 PTs)*

### LR — My Weakest Question Types
1. **Flaw** — missed 5x across both LR sections (diagnostic)
2. **Weaken / Sufficient Assumption / Necessary Assumption** — 3x each
3. **Conclusion / Supported** — recurring misses, including Level 1 questions

### RC — My Weakest Question Types
1. **Timing** — didn't read passages 3–4 at all on diagnostic
2. **Social Science passages** — weakest passage type (4/6 wrong on LawHub untimed)
3. **Vocabulary/density** — Law and Social Science passages slow reading significantly

### Common Mistake Categories
- [ ] Misidentifying the conclusion
- [ ] Falling for out-of-scope answer choices
- [ ] Mistaken reversal / negation (conditional logic)
- [ ] Missing the flaw because I agreed with the conclusion
- [ ] Going outside the passage on RC Inference questions
- [ ] Confusing what the passage *says* vs. what it *implies*
- [ ] Time pressure causing careless picks
