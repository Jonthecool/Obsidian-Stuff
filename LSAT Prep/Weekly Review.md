## Phase Transition Checkpoints

### End of Phase 1 (Week 4)
- [ ] I can identify the conclusion and premises in any LR stimulus
- [ ] I know all 10 LR question types and their attack strategies
- [ ] I can form the contrapositive of any conditional statement
- [ ] I can map RC passage structure before answering questions
- [ ] Diagnostic taken and baseline score recorded

### End of Phase 2 (Week 10)
- [ ] Accuracy has improved on my top 3 weakest question types
- [ ] I can finish a timed 35-min LR section with reasonable accuracy
- [ ] I can finish a timed 35-min RC section with reasonable accuracy
- [ ] Error log shows clear patterns I'm actively working on
- [ ] PT score is trending upward

### End of Phase 3 (Week 16)
- [ ] Target score hit on at least one PT
- [ ] Consistent scores across the last 3 PTs
- [ ] All major question type weaknesses addressed
- [ ] Test day logistics confirmed (test center or remote setup)
