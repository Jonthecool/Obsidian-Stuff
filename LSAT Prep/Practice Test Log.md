## After Every PT: Blind Review Checklist

- [ ] Finished the timed test — did not check answers yet
- [ ] Went back through every question and re-answered without time pressure
- [ ] For every question you changed: understand *why* your first answer was wrong
- [ ] For every question you were unsure on: look up the explanation
- [ ] Logged all missed questions in [[Error Log]]
- [ ] Identified top 2–3 patterns from this PT
- [ ] Added takeaways to [[Weekly Review]]
